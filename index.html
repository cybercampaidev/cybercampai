About Cybercamp.ai

We are setting up camp at the edge of two worlds.

Cybersecurity and artificial intelligence are no longer separate topics. Cybercamp.ai brings together the experience of defenders, developers, researchers, attackers and the people who set the rules.

We want a programme grounded in real experience, verifiable conclusions and an honest discussion of limitations. No magic tools. We always ask: how do we know a solution makes sense, and what would change our mind?

FAQ

Before we set out.

Who is Cybercamp.ai for?+

People working in cybersecurity, development, data, AI, governance and academia. The programme will accommodate different experience levels, with each session clearly marked.

What language will the programme use?+

We plan to host talks in both Czech and English. The language will always be shown directly in the schedule.

When will tickets and the final programme be available?+

We will announce the date, venue, ticket sales and call for papers gradually on this website and Cybercamp.ai social channels.
