About Cybercamp.ai

For people doing the work.

Cybersecurity and artificial intelligence are no longer separate topics. The programme focuses on useful experience, verifiable conclusions and open discussion.

Expect lessons from real projects, practical demonstrations and space to compare what works, what fails and where the limits are.

01

Security of AI

Building and operating safer models, agents, data pipelines and AI applications.

02

AI for Defense

Using AI for detection, response and security automation.

03

AI for Practitioners

Tools, workflows and lessons that improve day-to-day security work.

04

People & Governance

Accountability, regulation and practical risk decisions.

Organizers

The people behind the camp.

Cybercamp.ai is built by two co-organizers bringing the programme, partners and community together.

JB

Co-organizer

Jan Bleha

Jan Bleha is an experienced community builder and event strategist with more than a decade of experience connecting people across technology and cybersecurity. He has led the growth of DevConf.cz for over 1,200 attendees, coordinated hundreds of speakers and volunteers, and developed community and employer-branding initiatives for organizations including Red Hat, Kiwi.com, Whalebone, and Datamole.
As a co-organizer of Cybercamp.ai, Jan brings deep expertise in creating engaging events, building inclusive professional communities, and turning ambitious ideas into memorable experiences. Contact Jan
HN

Co-organizer

Hung Ngo

Hung Ngo is a cybersecurity specialist and the author of the KyberKecy podcast, he regularly speaks at industry events, and has spent more than 600 hours teaching and mentoring future cybersecurity professionals with Czechitas.
As a co-organizer of Cybercamp.ai, Hung brings together hands-on security experience, community building, and a talent for explaining complex subjects through accessible stories and nerdy humor. He is particularly interested in how AI can support security operations and practitioners, while also introducing new risks that organizations must learn to manage. Contact Hung
